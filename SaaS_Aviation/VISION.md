# Vision

Build the reference SaaS ERP for aviation inventory, purchasing, repairs, exchanges, leasing, RFQ, quotations, and stock lifecycle management.

The product should combine the operational seriousness of Pentagon2000 and Quantum Control, the usability discipline of Salesforce and Odoo Enterprise, and a modern SaaS architecture ready for multi-tenant aviation businesses.

## Product Promise

Every aviation object knows its context:

- a company knows contacts, stock, RFQs, quotes, POs, documents, users, and history;
- a part number knows stock, external availability, repairs, exchanges, alternates, aircraft applicability, quotes, certificates, and margin;
- a stock item knows owner, Tag Info, traceability, status, location, movement timeline, linked RFQs/quotes/POs, and documents.

## North Star

Reduce the time from customer demand to reliable quote, purchase, delivery, repair, exchange, or sale while improving traceability and auditability.
