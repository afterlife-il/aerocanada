# AI Roadmap

AI in this ERP is an operational assistant, not a generic chatbot.

## Future Capabilities

- Natural language inventory search.
- Supplier recommendation.
- Customer history analysis.
- Price prediction.
- Quote generation.
- RFQ generation.
- Margin suggestions.
- Repair prediction.
- Exchange/core tracking.
- Document and certificate analysis.
- Duplicate company/contact detection.
- Smart follow-up reminders.

## Guardrails

- AI cannot infer ownership.
- AI cannot execute stock movement without explicit user approval and audit log.
- AI answers must cite ERP records.
- Sensitive tenant data must remain tenant-scoped.
